Preparing and reviewing landscape shop drawings and as-built sheets for a large themed garden complex centered on an aviary dome, faux-rock tunnels and an elevated boardwalk.

Responsibilities spanned hardscape plans, coordinates, levels and grading, sections, and full submittal packages, alongside site plans coordinating natural and built elements across the wadi walk, water feature, and the individually themed gardens - Pilocene, Aviary, Butterfly, Carboniferous, Maze, Discovery.

Visualizations produced in AutoCAD, Revit, Civil 3D, SketchUp and Lumion supported technical reports, SCRs/RFIs and design justifications, with ongoing multi-disciplinary coordination (MEP, Structural, Architectural) to resolve clashes before site execution.
